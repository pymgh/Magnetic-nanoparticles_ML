Magnetic nanoparticles are widely used in hyperthermia treatments, where magnetic nanoparticles are used to heat tumor cells, thereby destroying the affected cells. Although this process destroys tumor tissues, it helps preserve healthy tissues.

In this work, experimental data related to hyperthermia were utilized, and different machine learning models were used to predict the specific absorption rate (SAR) of nanoparticles.
